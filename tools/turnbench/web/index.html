Model Provenance

Production Lua
Dubins turn generation, turn-end target and extension, tight-turn correction, sharp headland-corner generation, marker-based raising, forward/reverse lowering decisions and single-trailer heading integration.
Runtime parity is incomplete
CP's sharp-corner paths, single rear-implement finishing sequence and direction-switch controls are represented, but its PPC, collision-aware pathfinding, complete implement controller events, combine/chained-implement logic and live geometry are not. A completed playback is not an in-game feasibility result.
Test-only model
Flat ground, bounded-curvature pursuit, single passive trailer, rectangular working envelope and timed raising/lowering. Steering profiles supply illustrative radius/hitch values, not GIANTS vehicle physics. Plough turnover, collisions, slip and articulated chassis dynamics are not simulated.
Measurements
Complete-course playback does not report coverage totals; use central-pass tests for sampled entry and exit coverage. Missed coverage uses a 0.25 m cell-centre raster of the first 20 m of the incoming row and the last 20 m of the outgoing row. Final missed coverage is independent of playback time. Envelope depth is this run's maximum extent, not a certified minimum headland recommendation.
Comparison
Extra clearance increases the minimum turn-start distance into the headland; raising may already require more space than that minimum. It is an experiment, not an implemented Courseplay fix. The 45-degree case is synthetic, not a replay of the game log.
Calibration
PW 100-12 dimensions are approximate and partially based on the supplied log. Row exit uses CP's raise predicate with an approximate hydraulic timer; the rig waits until that timer expires before turning. The rectangular work envelope stays fully active until then: individual plough shares and progressive lifting are not modelled. Steering profiles are illustrative radius/hitch presets, not measurements of GIANTS tractors. Real-time logs of tractor, hitch and working markers are still needed for calibration.
Turn types
Dubins row turns include driven exit, turn and entry. Reeds-Shepp three-point turns and 90-degree headland loops use CP's route generators with planar movement. CP's analytic boundary fallback adds reversing when permitted; trailer steering uses AIReverseDriver's hitch correction. Complete courses include headlands, rows and connecting turns. GIANTS physics, traffic coordination, plough turnover and the game's full dynamic obstacle pathfinder remain outside this offline model.
